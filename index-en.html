From Within

From within communication work

Narrativa Social was born from experience with measurement, social listening, data classification, scenario analysis, and intelligence for communication.

In this work, the problems that now guide our services and products emerged: criteria difficult to maintain at scale, poorly systematized processes, and large volumes of information that require operational work.

Some of these problems need analysis. Others can become tools.

Leadership

Over 10 years in communication, analytics, and intelligence

Henrique Paludetto

Founder, Narrativa Social

Henrique Paludetto is a journalist with an MBA in Marketing and training in Data Science. He has worked for over 10 years in research, analytics, and intelligence applied to communication, and was Analytics & Intelligence Manager at Weber Shandwick.

His experience includes projects for brands like Ambev, Itaú, Johnson & Johnson / Kenvue, Heineken, Warner Bros. Discovery, BRF, Saint-Gobain, 99, Bristol Myers Squibb, and Dyson.

Product

Text classification at scale, with your criteria.

Tagarelo is Narrativa Social's first product.

You upload a database, define what you need to classify, and establish your criteria. Tagarelo applies that logic to the entire set and organizes the results for review and analysis.

AI applies criteria at scale. You define, review, and interpret.
